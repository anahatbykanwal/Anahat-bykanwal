🌿 Returns & Exchange Policy

At Anahat by Kanwal, your satisfaction is our utmost priority. If you receive a size that doesn't fit or an item that differs from your expectations, we gladly offer hassle-free exchanges under the following terms:

  • Exchange Period: Exchange claims must be logged within 7 days from the date of package delivery.
  • Product Condition: Articles must be unused, unwashed, unaltered, and returned with all original tag labels and signature packaging intact.
  • Color Variation Notice: Slight variations in color shade or fabric texture due to studio lighting or screen display settings do not qualify as defects.
  • Custom & Sale Items: Custom-stitched outfits, altered abayas, or items bought under "Final Sale" promotions are non-exchangeable unless defective.

How to Request an Exchange:

Step 01

Contact Support

Send a message on WhatsApp (+92 327 4518801) or email hello@anahatbykanwal.com with your Order ID & photos.

Step 02

Pack the Item

Repack the unwashed garment securely in its original Anahat gift box with attached tags.

Step 03

Courier Return

Dispatch the parcel via any local courier service to our Lahore studio or opt for reverse pickup.

🚛 Shipping & Delivery Information

We partner with premier courier services across Pakistan to ensure your handcrafted orders arrive safely and promptly.

  • Standard Delivery Timeline: Major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Gujranwala, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi) receive orders within 2 to 4 business days. Remote areas require 4 to 6 business days.
  • Shipping Charges: A nominal flat rate of Rs. 250 applies nationwide.
  • Free Shipping: All orders above Rs. 5,000 qualify for complimentary free delivery.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Available for all deliveries across Pakistan up to Rs. 30,000.

💳 Damaged Items & Refund Terms

Every piece undergoes strict quality inspections prior to dispatch. However, in the rare event that an item arrives damaged or incorrect:

  • Damaged Arrivals: Notify our care team within 24 hours of delivery with unboxing photos/videos. We will send a fresh replacement at zero additional shipping fee.
  • Monetary Refunds: Cash refunds are issued via direct bank transfer or JazzCash/EasyPaisa strictly in cases of manufacturing flaws or stock unavailability. Normal size exchanges are fulfilled via replacement or online store credit.
